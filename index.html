Becoming an ALT in Japan

Are you ready to embark on an exciting journey in Japan? As an Assistant Language Teacher (ALT) , you'll play a key role in English education, working alongside Japanese teachers to inspire students and make learning fun.

  1. What You'll Do:
    • Assist in English lessons
    • Encourage cultural exchange
    • Engage students with interactive activities
  2. Where You'll Work:
    • Elementary, junior high, or high schools
    • Urban and rural locations across Japan
  3. Who Can Apply:
    • Native or fluent English speakers
    • Passionate about education and culture

Quick Facts about Japanese Culture 🏯

Respect & Hierarchy Bowing is a key form of respect; teachers hold a high status in society.
Formality in Schools Teachers are addressed as "Sensei"; strict discipline is common.
Classroom Culture Students rarely speak out; lessons are more teacher-centered.
Seasonal Traditions Schools celebrate cherry blossoms (hanami), sports day, and cultural festivals.
