Note Before Submitting your Application

Salary: Range of 2.4 to 3.0 million yen annually for 12-month contracts
Location: All across Japan
Working conditions: Work Mondays to Fridays with scheduled teaching hours between 8 am and 5 pm (there will be a few times a year when schools will have special evenings or weekend events)
Requirements: Can evidence having at least 12 years of education where the language of instruction in all courses was English
